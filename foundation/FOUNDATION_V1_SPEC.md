# Tech-Orb Foundation v1

## Mission

Investigate whether known physics can be embedded in a deeper, higher-dimensional state structure with non-trivial invariants, projection rules, or topology that yields observable consequences beyond reparameterization. Foundation v1 is a research specification, not “Theory v1.”

## F0 — Four Core Hypotheses

### F0.1 Physics State Manifold

**Class:** HYPOTHESIS / PROVISIONAL

There may exist a mathematical state space `P` whose points represent complete physical states and whose trajectories encode admissible evolution.

Minimum deliverables: define state content, dimension or dimensional rule, admissible subsets, equivalence relation, differentiable/topological structure, and at least one coordinate-independent structure. Merely collecting quantities into a vector does not meet the hypothesis.

### F0.2 Projection / Effective Representation

**Class:** HYPOTHESIS / PROVISIONAL

Observed 3+1D metrics, fields, masses, and couplings may be effective representations of a deeper state.

Minimum deliverables: define the source state, target observables, projection map, chart transitions, gauge/equivalence structure, invariants, and a test distinguishing physical projection from coordinate choice.

### F0.3 Emergent Time

**Class:** HYPOTHESIS / PROVISIONAL

Temporal order and duration may emerge from relational state evolution, correlations, or information updates.

Minimum deliverables: reconstruct proper time, Lorentz time dilation, gravitational redshift/time dilation, causal ordering, and null structure. Conflict with verified relativistic clock behavior excludes the tested implementation.

### F0.4 Invariant / Constraint / Symmetry

**Class:** HYPOTHESIS + OPEN QUESTION / PROVISIONAL

A deeper invariant, constraint surface, equivalence class, symmetry, fixed point, or topological invariant may organize physical representations.

Method rule: define admissible transformations before claiming an invariant. Simple sums of information, energy, mass, space, and time are prohibited absent units, covariance, and a derivation.

## F1 — Taiji Candidate Topology

F1 is a separate candidate global-topology module, classified as ANSATZ. It is not required for the survival of F0 and cannot be treated as known cosmic topology. See `topology/TAIJI_DUAL_TOPOLOGY.md`.

## F2 — Formula / Quantity Geometry

F2 investigates geometry on spaces of formulas, quantities, models, or representations. It must define objects, morphisms/transformations, units, equivalences, metric or topology, and physical interpretation. Until it yields a non-trivial invariant and discriminating observable, it is an OPEN QUESTION / research tool rather than Foundation canon.

## Separation from Validators

HIA, HEM, Hubble-tension datasets, CLASS, and HyRec are independent validators. They test bridge candidates and implementations. A failed validator excludes only its declared claim, implementation, or subclass. A successful fit does not promote Foundation hypotheses without an explicit derived bridge.

## Foundation v1 Completion Criteria

Foundation v1 advances only when all four F0 items have:

1. explicit mathematical definitions;
2. compatibility with relevant established physics;
3. a non-gauge, coordinate-independent content claim;
4. at least one discriminating observable bridge;
5. a registered falsification test;
6. ledger and evidence entries with reproducible support.
