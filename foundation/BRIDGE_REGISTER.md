## Promotion Rule

A bridge may be promoted only after mathematical definition, known-limit recovery, reproducibility, a discriminating observable, falsification criteria, and an approved Decision Log entry. In particular, no magnetogenesis action may be promoted directly from narrative plausibility or AI agreement.
