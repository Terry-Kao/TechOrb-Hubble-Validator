# Falsification Matrix

## Priority Attacks

| Priority | Target | Attack | Pass condition | Failure consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Projection hypothesis | Prove whether the proposed projection has coordinate/gauge-independent content and produces an observable not obtainable by reparameterization. | Explicit map, invariant, equivalence analysis, and discriminating observable. | Exclude the tested projection implementation; if every admissible construction reduces to relabeling, challenge F0.2. |
| 2 | Emergent-time hypothesis | Derive proper time, Lorentz/gravitational time dilation, causal order, and null structure; compare with precision clock/propagation constraints. | Covariant recovery in known regimes with declared approximation error. | Exclude the tested emergent-time implementation immediately on verified contradiction. |
| 3 | Foundation-to-cosmology bridge | Derive a stable physical action/closure through perturbations and observable spectra, then confront a full likelihood without target-value tuning. | Reproducible theory-to-observable chain, stable evolution, correct baseline, and valid likelihood. | Exclude the bridge/implementation or model subclass; do not automatically falsify Tech-Orb. |
