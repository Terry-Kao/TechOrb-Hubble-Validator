## 1. Research question

> Can the experienced 3+1-dimensional spacetime be a stable effective geometry and causal structure arising from a global constrained relational quantum structure that assumes neither fundamental classical space nor external/meta-time, after an internal quantum reference system is selected and conditioning, coarse-graining, and a quantum-to-classical transition are applied?

This is a research question, not a theory claim. The active candidate is a **Global Constrained Relational Quantum Structure / Relational Quantum Foundation**. Constructing a toy model does not establish that nature uses it.

## 2. Foundation claim vocabulary

Every new Foundation claim must use exactly one of these labels:

- `ESTABLISHED`: a mature result within a stated domain, or a directly verified repository fact.
- `COMPATIBLE`: not presently in conflict within the stated domain; neither support nor uniqueness.
- `HYPOTHESIS`: a falsifiable physical proposition not established by current evidence.
- `ANSATZ`: an exploratory mathematical/model choice without first-principles derivation.
- `REJECTED-LITERAL-VERSION`: wording or a literal physical interpretation retired from active use.

Legacy ledger labels are preserved for historical traceability. For new Foundation records, `ESTABLISHED` is the Foundation profile of a scoped legacy `FACT`; `HYPOTHESIS` and `ANSATZ` retain their ordinary meanings; `COMPATIBLE` must never be promoted to evidence; and `REJECTED-LITERAL-VERSION` is a bounded rejection of wording/interpretation, not a universal no-go result.

Hard rules:

1. Analogy is not evidence.
2. A constructible toy model is not evidence that nature instantiates it.
3. Compatibility with GR or QM is not support from GR or QM.
4. Every Foundation claim must have an explicit fail/falsification condition or remain a concept note outside the registry.
5. No gate PASS establishes a downstream layer. Each layer requires a separately frozen protocol and verdict.

## 3. Working ontology

The minimum working ontology is

\[
\mathfrak Q=(\mathcal H,\mathcal A,\rho,\mathcal K,\mathcal T),
\]

where:

- `H` is a state/Hilbert space (or a future justified generalization);
- `A` is an observable/operator algebra;
- `rho` is the global state, including the pure-state special case `|Psi><Psi|`;
- `K` is the set of physical-state, gauge, and Hamiltonian constraints;
- `T` is the allowed set of transformations/channels.

The ontology is a `HYPOTHESIS`. Any tensor factorization such as

\[
\mathcal H=\mathcal H_C\otimes\mathcal H_R\otimes\mathcal H_S\otimes\mathcal H_E
\]

is an `ANSATZ`, may be non-unique, and must not be mistaken for a fact about nature. Fundamental-level external or meta-time is prohibited. Parameters used to index conditional states are internal clock readings, not an external evolution variable.

## 4. One-way governance

The only authorized evidential direction is:

\[
\text{Foundation}\rightarrow\text{effective model}\rightarrow\text{validators}.
\]

- Foundation defines candidate quantum relations, constraints, reference choices, emergence maps, and their fail conditions.
- An effective model derives an explicit, scoped dynamics/geometry/observable interface from those definitions.
- Validators test the derived interface against mathematics, known limits, reproducibility, and observations.

HEM, Hubble data, CLASS, HyRec, cosmological anomalies, or a good fit may not define the Foundation in reverse. Validator success cannot promote a Foundation hypothesis without a separately derived bridge. Validator failure excludes only its declared claim, implementation, or subclass unless a Foundation-level proof establishes broader scope.

QF-0D FAILS if it cannot form a causal order/cone, yields only Euclidean geometry, requires a preferred external clock/frame, gives uncontrolled contradictory reference-dependent predictions, produces prohibited causal cycles, or permits operational superluminal signalling. QF-0D PASS does not establish QF-1. A PASS at QF-0A does not establish QF-0B/C/D or any later layer, the full ontology, or any statement about nature. ## 8. Global falsification gates

Each gate is independently necessary. A PASS means only that the named gate passed under its frozen scope.

| Gate | Required showing | Explicit FAIL condition |
|---|---|---|
| Quantum | Valid states, observables, probabilities, composition, normalization, and allowed dynamics/channels | Positivity/normalization fails, predictions are undefined, or a classical network is substituted for required quantum structure without derivation |
| Reference | Internal reference/clock construction and transformations yield controlled operational equivalence | Predictions depend arbitrarily on reference choice with no transformation law, equivalence class, or bounded explanation |
| Geometry | Relational data reconstruct a coordinate-independent metric/pseudometric or clearly named alternative | Reconstruction needs hidden coordinates/dimension, violates its preregistered axioms beyond tolerance, or is only visualization |
| Dimension | Independent estimators recover blind synthetic dimension with calibrated uncertainty | Answer is presupposed/tuned to 3, estimators are uncalibrated, or finite-size/noise behavior defeats the frozen thresholds |
| Causality | A stable Lorentzian causal order, null structure, and no-signalling behavior emerge in scope | Euclidean/inconsistent signature, causal cycles where prohibited, superluminal signalling, or unexplained preferred frame |
| GR | Relevant local Lorentz, equivalence, conservation, and Einstein/declared modified limit are derived | GR behavior is assumed instead of derived, or precision tests are contradicted in the claimed domain |
| Classicality | Geometry-like observables become robust and operationally redundant records under a stated error model | Decoherence/redundancy metrics fail, records are inaccessible/non-independent, or outcome/objectivity claims exceed what was tested |
| Information | Information measures and encodings are operationally defined and gauge-safe | Gauge redundancy is counted as physical information, encoding comparisons are undefined, or unitarity/replacement principle fails |
| Predictivity | At least one identifiable, non-gauge, discriminating prediction follows with a frozen decision rule | Proposal is pure reparameterization, has unlimited post-hoc freedom, or no observable can in principle distinguish it |
| Validator Leakage | Hidden generator/answers and validator outcomes cannot enter Foundation construction or reconstruction | Coordinates, target dimension, HEM fit, anomaly target, or test labels leak into model selection/tuning |
| Analogy | Every analogy is isolated from the evidential chain | A conclusion depends on shadow, embedding, Taiji, Buddhist, or other analogy as physical evidence |
| Novelty | Literature collision check identifies equivalence, prior art, and the actual residual claim | Claim is already known/relabelled, conflicts with an established result without resolution, or novelty cannot be stated after collision review |

Thresholds for executable gates must be frozen before result inspection. A threshold or metric altered after inspection invalidates the run; the outcome is not PASS.
