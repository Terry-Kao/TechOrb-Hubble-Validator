# Mathematical and Physical Requirements

## MR-1 — State Space

Define the set/category of states, regularity, dimensionality, boundary/singularity treatment, physical equivalence relation, and observables. State whether a manifold is necessary; stratified spaces, bundles, stacks, graphs, or other structures must not be excluded by vocabulary alone.

## MR-2 — Transformations and Invariants

Specify allowed transformations, composition, identity/inverse where applicable, gauge redundancy, and invariants. An invariant claim without a transformation group/class is incomplete.

## MR-3 — Projection

Define a map from deeper states to effective observables, its domain/codomain, locality/non-locality, degeneracy, invertibility, transition rules, and what is physically lost. Demonstrate that any claimed effect is not a coordinate or gauge artifact.

## MR-4 — Dynamics

Declare the evolution law: action/Hamiltonian/constraint flow/stochastic rule or another precise structure. Address well-posedness, initial/boundary data, conservation/constraints, stability, and known limits. A “dynamic transformation matrix” is only a placeholder until these are satisfied.

## MR-5 — Dimensions and Covariance

Every equation must be dimensionally consistent and state its units. Transformation laws must respect the relevant covariance and gauge structure. Unlike quantities cannot be added merely for conceptual symmetry.

## MR-6 — Emergent Time Recovery

An implementation must reproduce proper time, Lorentz transformations, gravitational time dilation/redshift, causal ordering, null propagation, and clock-comparison observations in its claimed domain.

## MR-7 — Quantum and Statistical Consistency

Where applicable, specify state probabilities/amplitudes, normalization, unitarity or replacement principle, locality/no-signalling behavior, entropy/information definitions, and the classical limit.

## MR-8 — Cosmological Interfaces

Any cosmological bridge must state background and perturbation equations, initial conditions, species content, neutrino assumptions, recombination model, gauge, stability, and observable calculation. It must distinguish `r_s(z_*)` from the BAO drag ruler `r_d = r_s(z_d)`.

## MR-9 — Identifiability and Novelty

Show that the proposal is not equivalent to a field redefinition or ordinary coordinate change. Identify at least one observable that separates it from the reference model under a declared likelihood or error model.

## MR-10 — Falsifiability

State the scope, test statistic/observable, tolerance or decision rule, and outcomes that exclude a CLAIM, IMPLEMENTATION, or SUBCLASS. “Consistent so far” is not verification.
