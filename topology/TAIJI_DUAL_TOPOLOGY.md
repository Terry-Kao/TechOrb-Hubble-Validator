# Taiji Dual Topology — Candidate Module F1

**Epistemic class:** ANSATZ  
**Evidence status:** PROVISIONAL

## Bounded Statement

F1 explores whether a global state structure with two complementary sectors and a branch-exchange operation can be formulated mathematically and connected to physical observables. “Taiji” is a geometric heuristic, not evidence that the universe has this topology.

## Candidate Ingredients

- sectors or sheets `M+` and `M-`;
- an exchange map or involution `J: M+ ↔ M-`;
- candidate relations to orientation reversal, time reversal, CPT, analytic extension, or mirror sectors;
- gluing/boundary/fixed-point structure;
- compatibility with local causal and Lorentzian physics.

These candidates are alternatives, not established equivalences. Black-hole/white-hole language is an interpretation candidate only and must not be presented as known interior physics.

## Minimum Formalization

1. Define spaces, topology, differentiable structure, and singular/boundary sets.
2. Define `J`, including whether it is continuous, smooth, involutive, orientation reversing, antiunitary, or none of these.
3. Define physical states and observables on each sector.
4. State local recovery conditions and global conservation/constraint laws.
5. Identify a consequence not equivalent to coordinate doubling.
6. Register an executable falsification test.

## Advancement Rule

F1 remains ANSATZ until a coherent mathematical construction satisfies known physics and yields a discriminating observable. Failure of F1 does not by itself exclude the four F0 hypotheses.
